Open source on solid ground.

Basalt is the rock beneath almost everything: the ocean floor, roads and pavements, and, over time, some of the most fertile soil there is. OpenBasalt builds open infrastructure software in the same spirit.

Principles

Three ideas came from the rock.

When basalt cools slowly it splits by itself into hexagonal columns: independent pieces that together make solid ground. That is what we want our software to be.

  1. Foundation

    Infrastructure tools that everything else stands on: directory, DNS, security, the system itself. Things nobody notices while they work.

  2. Columns

    Independent projects, each with its own purpose and release cycle, that fit together like basalt's hexagonal columns.

  3. Fertile soil

    Weathered basalt becomes some of the most fertile soil there is. Open code, exposed to time and people, becomes ground for others to grow.

Open, because everything here is open. Basalt, because it is the base.

Basalt OS

Pre-alpha

A Linux distribution based on Fedora, built security and AI first. AI agents can do real work on the system through typed, auditable actions, always confined and confirmed by a person. When the two pull apart, security sets the limits.

Working in the source today

  • SELinux enforcing on every install, and disk encryption with LUKS2 unlocked by the TPM2.
  • A snapshot before and after every package change, with rollback from the running system or the boot menu.
  • Secure Boot, and signed packages and repository metadata.
  • A confined local assistant that proposes fixes and applies nothing without your confirmation.
  • A desktop shell prototype where every change waits for the person.

In development

  • A desktop edition with voice, where AI uses your apps for you, safely.
  • Approvals from a phone paired with your machine.
  • A live ISO, to boot and look around without installing anything.

Nothing is released as stable yet. Today you can build the installer from the source and try it in a virtual machine. The details, the roadmap and a way to tell us what you think are on the Basalt OS site.

Samba Conductor

Pre-release

A web administrator and self-service portal for Samba Active Directory, with encrypted domain backups, file servers and Google Workspace sync as companion projects. RPM packages are in the basalt-tools repository on obpkg.org.

Giving back

Built on the work of others.

Basalt OS stands on Fedora and its community. Samba Conductor exists because of Samba. Every project here depends on years of open source work by people we may never meet, and on many distributions and projects that solve problems in their own way. We learn from all of them.

We try new ideas in the open, publish what we build under open licenses, and send what belongs upstream back where it came from. Basalt OS is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Fedora Project or Red Hat.
